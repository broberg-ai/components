# F052.35 — In fullscreen on a phone the pain panel is below the fold

## Why
Measured 27/10 2026 with Lens (chromium, iphone-15) on `packages/bodymap/demo/f052-31-34.tsx` (fullscreen, hint at stage-bottom), flow 64b3062c: tapping the chest selects CHEST and hides the hint as intended, but `bodymap3d-close` and all eleven `bodymap3d-intensity-*` buttons sit at `top >= window.innerHeight` (852). The canvas is 369×702 and fills the fullscreen surface; the panel column wraps below it — the same mechanism F052.31 found for the hint. Consequence: in fullscreen a patient can mark a body part but cannot choose an intensity, close the panel, or remove the mark from the panel.

Same family as F052.27 / F052.31 / F052.33: a control that exists in the DOM and passes every presence test, and cannot be reached.

## Open questions (before building)
1. Does fd-sundhed's production page show this, or does their container differ? (asked 27/10)
2. Layout choice: overlay the panel over the lower part of the stage (sheet), or shrink the canvas while a region is open? The owner/consumer may have a view; a sheet keeps the body large.

## Scope
- Fullscreen only. The boxed layout is unchanged (negative control).
- The panel must be fully inside the viewport and reachable (elementFromPoint), with the body still visible.

## Non-goals
- No change to the panel's content or to decidePick.

## Reuse
No shared primitive; bodymap owns its layout.

## Tests
A Lens manuscript in the repo, run against the demo page, that goes red if the panel is below the fold again. happy-dom cannot render this (no WebGL).

## Rollout
Patch release of @broberg/bodymap, release report to fd-sundhed.
