# F052.34 — Round the intensity buttons and the close control

**Christian**, relayed by fd-sundhed:

> *«alt andet er rundt — tror det ville se endnu mere lækkert ud.»*

## Today

Rounded squares, and **«10» is wider than the other ten**. `three.tsx` uses
`minWidth: 30, height: 30` with the shared button radius, so a two-digit label
stretches its own button: the row is eleven shapes in two widths.

F052.17 already centred the digits for exactly this reason. It fixed the
alignment and left the width.

## The sizing rule is the whole card

**Dimension the circle by the WIDEST label, not the average one.**

Otherwise «10» becomes an ellipse among circles — which is *worse* than eleven
rounded squares, because the odd one out goes from slightly wider to visibly a
different shape. Fixed `width === height`, chosen so «10» fits at the shipped
font size, applied to all eleven.

## The touch target is a constraint, not a detail

F052.8 established **≥44px hit areas** for this component because it is primarily
a phone surface, and F052.11 made accessibility a **legal** duty here (Aalborg
Kommune).

A circle is visually smaller than the square that contained it at the same box
size. So shrinking the box to make «10» sit neatly could take the tappable area
below the floor without anything looking wrong.

**The visible shape may shrink. The hit area may not.**

## The close control

Same request, one control over. `bodymap3d-close` is a rounded rectangle; every
other close in their app is round. It already carries a 40×40 treatment in the
fullscreen state (F052.27) — this makes the panel's own close match.

## Reuse

Nothing to import. The colours already come from `ui.accent` / `ui.accentText`
after F052.30, so this is geometry alone — which is exactly why it is a small
card: the theming work is done, and a shape change now touches no colour.

## Non-goals

- New props or palette fields. Presentation only.
- Any behaviour change. The pick logic, the gating and the outcomes are settled
  in F052.20, F052.26 and F052.32.
- Rounding the sex toggle or the quality pills. Not asked for; the pills are
  already `borderRadius: 999`.
