# F079.6 — retention and redaction

**Status:** INTERIM · not started · **Epic:** F079 (`@broberg/chat`)
**Written:** 2026-09-11 (dansk tid) · **Filed against by:** helpdesk

> **THIS IS AN INTERIM PLAN-DOC.** Scope is not settled. It exists because three
> sibling plan-docs already promise this contract, and a forward-reference with
> nothing behind it is a lie told to the next reader. Open questions are at the
> top, where this repo's own rule says to put them.

## Open questions — answer before any code

1. **Where does redaction live?** `@broberg/chat`, `@broberg/secret-scan`, or its
   own package. Leaning **its own**: neither chat's consumers nor secret-scan's
   are the whole audience, and secret-scan's dependency-free *secrets-only*
   contract is worth not diluting.
2. **What shape is the context argument** that lets names be looked up rather than
   guessed? helpdesk's version resolves the case's own participants. A shared
   package needs a form that does not assume there is a case.

## Why this card exists at all

**F079.6 was a phantom.** Three plan-docs reference it:

There was no card and no doc. A reader of F079.3 is told a contract exists that

naming it: the right defect through the wrong door.

## The measurement that makes it urgent

`@broberg/secret-scan` 0.9.3, on a Danish support message with CPR, e-mail,
phone and a name:

```
findings   []
redacted   byte-identical to the input
hasSecret  false
```

**That is correct behaviour.** It scans secrets and credentials, and its Discovery
one-liner says “Pure, dependency-free secret/credential redaction”.

**The defect is that the result carries no denominator of AXES.** `findings: []`
and “that whole dimension is not something I examine” are the same object. And
secret-scan is the **first hit on Discovery for `redaction`** — so a consumer
searching for exactly this lands on a green answer and sends personal data into a
model context.

Same shape as F005.18's fix in `@broberg/mail` this morning, one level up: not
*“this check did not run”* but *“this dimension is not checked at all”*.

## The split — helpdesk's, accepted

| | what it is | where it can live |
|---|---|---|
| **Redaction** | text → text | storage-free, dependency-free — chat's own constraints |
| **Retention** | deleting rows | **cannot** live in a storage-free package |

**Writing down that `@broberg/chat` will NEVER own retention is the valuable
half.** A consumer waiting for something that is never coming is worse off than
one told to build it. That sentence belongs in the package's README, not only
here.

## What helpdesk offers (role: `src`)

- **The `\b` at BOTH ends of the CPR pattern is the guard.** The mutation that
  reorders it against the phone pattern went **GREEN** — ordering never protected
  anything. Without the boundaries a personnummer becomes *unreadable rather than
  removed*: remnants that still identify and look redacted. A
  fix-that-hides-the-damage, and the class where a green test is most dangerous.
- **Names are looked up, not guessed.** “Mette” and “Mandag” are one shape to a
  regex. They resolve the case's own addresses — `mette.jensen@fd.dk →
  ["mette","jensen"]`, `support@fd.dk → []` because a role address is not a
  person.
- **Their ragged edge, raised unprompted:** the sign-off pattern collapses
  `Med venlig hilsen\nLars Hansen` onto one line. Fix before sharing.

## Reuse

Discovery checked per F217 — `redaction`, `pii`, `retention`. First hit is
`@broberg/secret-scan`, and the point of this card is that it is the wrong answer
to that search while looking like the right one. `@broberg/logger` (second hit)
delegates to it and inherits the same blind spot for anything logging user text.

Nothing to adopt, and the reuse finding is the inverse of the usual one — not
*“someone already built this”* but *“something already answers this question
wrongly, confidently, and first.”*

## Non-goals

- **Retention inside `@broberg/chat`.** Permanent, not “not yet”.
- **Guessing names from shape.** A regex cannot tell Mette from Monday, and a
  redactor that tries will both miss people and mangle prose.
