# F052.32 — A tap held longer than 450 ms is thrown away, in silence

**Christian, via fd-sundhed:**

> *«det kan da ikke passe at det er så svært … når man kan placere en markering på
> en krop og fjerne den igen med en knap, kan man vel også fjerne den igen ved at
> trykke på det samme område — det er vel ikke rocket science.»*

He is right, and the mechanism has been there since 0.2.6.

## The diagnosis was one line too low

fd-sundhed wrote: *"det ser ud til at 3D-renderens tap-håndtering vælger «åbn
regionen» før den spørger."* Reasonable, and not what happens. In order:

```ts
// three.tsx:594
if (Math.hypot(e.clientX - downX, e.clientY - downY) > 6 || Date.now() - downT > 450) return;   // ← HERE
const k = pick(e.clientX, e.clientY);
const outcome = pickRef.current(k);      // → decidePick → "clear"
```

`decidePick` is correct and *is* reached — when the tap gets that far. **It does
not.** The guard above returns from the whole handler: no pick, no clear, no
`emitFeedback`, no repaint. **Nothing at all.**

## Why 450 ms is the wrong test

The guard exists to tell a tap from a drag. **The distance check already does that
job completely** — a drag moves.

Time adds nothing to that distinction. What it adds is a rule that *a slow press is
not a tap*. But a slow press is exactly what a **careful** tap is: aiming at a
small body part on a phone, at a mark you are trying to hit again, takes longer
than a casual one.

So the guard discards precisely the taps a user is trying hardest to make — on a
health app whose users include people in pain.

## It explains both of his earlier reports, which we closed as "nothing was broken"

| report | actual cause |
|---|---|
| *«fjern-knappen er væk»* | nothing happened, so the panel and button stayed as they were |
| *«lyden virker ikke»* | `emitFeedback` is BELOW the guard — a swallowed tap emits nothing |

**Both were the same swallowed tap, reported from two directions, and we explained
each away separately.** That is the expensive part: we had two independent
sightings of one defect and treated each as a misunderstanding.

## The general shape

**A swallowed tap is indistinguishable from a broken component.** The rejection
path is silent, so from the user's chair *"I tapped and nothing happened"* is the
only available reading — and it is also what a genuinely broken component looks
like.

Same family as everything else this week: an absence that reads as a result.

## Not yet measured, and it matters

fd-sundhed reproduced this **with Lens**, whose synthetic clicks are fast — so the
time guard should *not* have fired in their run.

Either their repro was a real finger, or **there is a second cause**. Asked
directly rather than assumed, because two causes behind one symptom is how the
wrong one gets fixed and the right one survives.

Most likely second candidate: a **controlled `value`** that the parent does not
write back on removal — the removal would then be reverted on the next render, and
the report would look untouched.

## Why this had no test

The guard lives in the WebGL pointer path that happy-dom cannot execute — the same
gap as F052.23 and F052.25, and the same fix: extract the decision as a pure
function so the suite can reach it (the `decidePick` precedent).

That is three defects in one file found in the same place, which is itself worth
noting: **the untestable path is where the untested bugs are**, and that is not a
coincidence.

## Reuse

Discovery searched `tap`, `gesture`, `pointer`, `touch`. Nothing in `@broberg/*`
owns gesture recognition, and one primitive for it would be premature — this is a
single threshold in one renderer.

Internally, the fix follows the shape this package has now used three times:
extract the decision, have the real path call it, assert the invariant, mutate
every site.

## Non-goals

- Changing `decidePick` or the remove-button gating. Both correct, both confirmed
  twice, and the bug is above them.
- Removing the distance check. It is the one doing real work.
- A long-press gesture. Nobody asked for one, and adding a meaning to a long press
  is what created this problem.
