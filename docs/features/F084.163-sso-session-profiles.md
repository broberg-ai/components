# F084.163 — @broberg/sso session profiles + the hourly silent check

> Story under F084. Priority medium. Effort M. Status: backlog — planned, not ordered yet.

## Motivation
BID card F084.7 («Log ud overalt») has four requirements that live in the CLIENT, not in BID. The owner approved the numbers 16/9 (Decision Register D-38a092): app standard 7 days rolling · sensitive data 12 hours + 30 minutes of inactivity · a silent check against BID every hour (the net under back-channel logout: an app whose logout endpoint is down is logged out within an hour at most) · BID's own account surface 1 hour (BID already has that).

Measured in @broberg/sso 0.18.2 (13/10): the 7 days exist (`DEFAULT_SESSION_MAX_AGE`, config.ts:75) but are ABSOLUTE, not rolling; 12 h exists only as an env value each app must remember (`SSO_SESSION_MAX_AGE=43200`); 30 min inactivity does not exist; the silent check does not exist (prompt=none is a full top-level redirect only, F084.6); no test stops an adapter from setting its own number.

## Design (agreed with broberg-id, intercom #3117 → #3122 → #3123)
- **One knob:** `sessionProfile: "standard" | "sensitive"`. The numbers are constants in sso, never env. A test fails if an adapter or env sets its own.
- **standard:** 7 days, rolling (renewed on use).
- **sensitive:** 12 hours absolute + 30 minutes idle.
- **Silent check = RENEWAL, not a new endpoint.** sso asks for `offline_access` at login and keeps the refresh token in the session (encrypted, server-side). Every hour: `grant_type=refresh_token` against `/oauth2/token` with `credentials: "omit"`. BID already refuses renewal when the BID login has ended — logout, «log ud overalt», new password, expiry (BID F084.156, in production). Refused → delete the app session. Accepted → store the rotated token. BID down (5xx/network) → keep the session (never log out on a hiccup — same rule as web-clipper's auth).

### Rejected (and why — do not re-propose)
- **A `prompt=none` redirect every hour:** it navigates the user away mid-work.
- **Token introspection:** public clients (most of the fleet) cannot introspect, and BID answers `active` only to the client that got the token.
- **A new «is session X alive» endpoint in BID:** needs client auth to not become a session-guessing oracle; renewal already gives the same answer with what exists.

## Scope
- sso: `sessionProfile`, constants, rolling renewal, idle expiry, encrypted server-side refresh-token storage, hourly renewal, deletion on refusal.
- Tests with a fake clock and a fake BID; read back from the stored session.
- Release + report (D-4d0783).

### Non-goals
- BID's own account surface (BID has it).
- BID's side: giving each app registration `offline_access` — broberg-id does that app by app once sso is ready.

## Reuse
Extends `@broberg/sso` itself (the fleet's one BID client). No new package.

## Dependencies
- broberg-id: `offline_access` per app registration (they add it on our signal).
- BID F084.7 points at this card; its AC#4/#8 are proven on BID's side.

## Rollout
Behind the profile: `standard` is the default, so an app that does nothing gets rolling 7 days (a change from absolute 7 days — say so in the release report). An app holding health data sets `sensitive`. The hourly check only runs when a refresh token exists, so apps without `offline_access` keep today's behaviour until broberg-id enables it.
